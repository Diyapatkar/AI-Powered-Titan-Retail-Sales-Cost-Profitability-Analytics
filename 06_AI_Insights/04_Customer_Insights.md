The report suggests an opportunity to encourage promising customers to make repeat purchases and re-engage customers classified as at-risk.

## Suggested Strategies
- **Loyal customers:** reward points, early access, relevant offers, and loyalty benefits.
- **At-risk customers:** personalized follow-ups, service reminders, carefully targeted comeback offers.
- **New/promising customers:** post-purchase support, product care information, relevant recommendations, and incentives for a second purchase.

## Important Validation
The companion `AI_Business_Insights.md` source says customer-level fields, repeat-purchase information, and RFM analysis are unavailable, while the DOCX report provides segment counts. Confirm the source CSV, customer identifiers, RFM calculation, and segment definitions before treating these counts as validated results.

Useful additional fields: Customer ID, transaction history, frequency, acquisition source, loyalty status, campaign response, and customer lifetime value.
