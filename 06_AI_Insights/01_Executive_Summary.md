# Executive Summary
## Titan-Inspired Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics

> Portfolio project using simulated data only. It is not actual Titan Company Limited data.

## Overview
The simulated dataset reports **10,564 delivered orders**, **15,380 units**, **₹35.79 crore revenue**, **₹6.64 crore profit**, and an **18.55% overall profit margin**. The key opportunity is to grow sustainable profit rather than revenue alone.

## Key Highlights
- **Revenue:** ₹35.79 Cr
- **Cost:** ₹29.15 Cr
- **Profit:** ₹6.64 Cr
- **Profit margin:** 18.55%
- **Highest revenue category:** Jewellery
- **Highest absolute-profit category:** Watches
- **Highest regional revenue:** West
- **Highest regional profit and margin:** South (in the category/regional analysis supplied)
- **Highest monthly revenue:** March 2026

## Main Business Priorities
1. Investigate Jewellery's comparatively low margin while protecting its large revenue contribution.
2. Protect Watches profitability and availability.
3. Explore measured growth in high-margin, smaller categories such as Eyewear and Accessories.
4. Review monthly volatility and regional performance differences.
5. Investigate loss-making transactions and monitor revenue, cost, profit and margin together.

## Data Note
The uploaded source reports differ on whether discount and customer-level analysis are available. Customer segmentation and discount metrics are therefore treated as provisional and should be reconciled with the actual dataset and analysis outputs before being used as final claims.
