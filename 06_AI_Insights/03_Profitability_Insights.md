## Findings
- Watches has the largest absolute category profit.
- Jewellery has the largest revenue base but comparatively low margin.
- Eyewear and Accessories show high margins on smaller revenue bases; margin alone is not enough to conclude they can scale profitably.
- Transaction-level profit ranges from **−₹28,794.24** to **₹88,742.67** in the report, with mean profit **₹6,282.59** and median **₹4,304.27**. Review loss-making transactions for possible cost, pricing, discount, returns, or data issues.

## Actions
- Review Jewellery product costs, supplier terms, fulfilment/shipping costs, and price points.
- Protect Watches availability and avoid unnecessary discounting.
- Evaluate high-margin categories using incremental profit and sales-volume tests.
- Track profit and margin alongside revenue.
